How we work

Map it. Find the waste. Build the opportunity.

Whether it's tendering, invoicing or a fully bespoke build, every project starts the same way: understanding how your business actually runs today.

Step 1

Map your workflow

We sit down with you and your team and map the whole journey: enquiry, tender or quote, job, invoice, payment. Every handover, every system, every spreadsheet.

It starts with a free 15-minute call to see if it's worth doing.
Step 2

Find the waste

We pin down where time and money leak out: details typed in twice, quotes waiting on one person, invoices sent late, payments nobody is chasing.

You get a clear picture of what each problem is costing you, in hours and dollars.
Step 3

Size the opportunity

We rank the fixes by what they're worth to you and what they cost to build, then agree a scope together.

Clear scope, clear pricing, clear timeline. No vague "it depends."
Step 4

Build and go live

We build it, connect it to your existing tools, test it on real jobs and hand it over running. You deal directly with the person who built it.

Most builds go live within 1–⁠3 weeks, depending on complexity.
